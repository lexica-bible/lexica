# TICKET — Lexica-minted numbers for numberless PN identities (DEFERRED)

Opened 2026-07-28 at JP's suggestion, deferred by agreement pending lane-#3
experience. Do not build without a fresh ruling.

## The idea

~14,850 PN identities are lemma-only — real Greek name-forms (mostly LXX-only
names: Καϊνάν, Μαθουσαλά, the Chronicles genealogies) that Strong's never
numbered (NT-only scheme) and even STEP's extensions don't cover. Lexica could
mint its own identifiers, the way STEP minted G9xxx.

## Conditions recorded at open (reviewer)

- **Namespace discipline:** a distinct prefix that can never collide with
  Strong's or STEP ranges (e.g. LX0001-style); provenance visible on every card
  — a reader must always be able to tell a Lexica identifier from Strong's.
- **Need test first:** lane #3 makes the lemma itself a working Word-study key
  (PN:<form>, live). Mint numbers only if a concrete need shows up — a join
  that wants them, or lemma-spelling instability. "Just the links" is already
  solved cheaper.
- **If built:** cert-style data lane — mint from the identity table (one number
  per IDENTITY, not per lemma — person/place distinction matters), pre-registered
  counts, dry-run, and the assignment table is append-only (numbers never reused
  or reassigned).

Status: DEFERRED — revisit after a few weeks of lemma-keyed Word study.
